Improving Purpose Alignment

It looks like your employees are having a hard time picturing how to align their individual purpose with your company’s goals. There could be a few reasons for this so let’s dig deeper.

Improperly aligned Incentives – Employees are not rewarded for behaviors that advance the company’s mission.

Lack of Communication from Upper Management – Employees do not understand what they are supposed to be working towards for the firm. What are the firm’s goals, and how does each employee contribute to meeting those goals?

Unclear Motivation – How does the firm’s success result in the employee’s success? What communication and mechanisms are in place to make sure the employees are incentivized to accomplish the company’s goals?
